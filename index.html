Since Eddie's days

A family workshop, Penrith-proud.

What we do

One stop for engine & electrical.

From a squealing belt on a Saturday morning to a year-long electrical gremlin nobody else could find — this is the work we love.

Engine Diagnostics

Warning lights, misfires, power loss. We go beyond the fault code with scopes, live data and pressure testing.

Auto Electrical
