Our story

Old-school service. New-school tools.

Eddie's Workshop has been part of Penrith for decades. Today it's run by Matt Azzopardi — the mechanic who worked alongside Eddie for years before taking over the keys.

The workshop

Built on trust, one car at a time.

Eddie built the workshop's reputation the old-fashioned way: being honest, going above and beyond, and treating customers like friends. Whole families — and their parents — have brought their cars here for 20, 25, even 35 years.

When Eddie retired, his chief mechanic Matt Azzopardi took over. Matt had worked behind the scenes for Eddie for over five years, so the values stayed exactly the same — while the diagnostic gear got a serious upgrade.

Trading as ZIC Service Centre Pty Ltd, we're a family-owned and operated team of mechanics who will always go above and beyond to help with your automotive needs.
