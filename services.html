Engine Diagnostics

Diagnostics is what we're known for. A fault code tells you where to start looking — not what to replace. We use scan tools, live data, oscilloscopes and pressure testing to find the real cause, so you don't pay for parts you didn't need.

  • Check-engine & warning lights
  • Misfires & rough running
  • Loss of power / limp mode
  • In-cylinder pressure testing
  • Intermittent & hard-to-find faults
  • Second opinions on other quotes
From our workshop

A Holden Captiva came in with oil getting into the coolant — we diagnosed it thoroughly before touching a part.

02 / 11

Auto Electrical

We're fully qualified auto electricians. From a single broken wire to a full vehicle rewire, we trace electrical faults methodically — including CAN-bus networks, airbag, ABS and sensor circuits.

  • Wiring & harness repairs
  • Full vehicle rewires
  • CAN-bus diagnostics
  • Airbag, ABS & sensor faults
  • Alternators & starter motors
  • Battery testing (carbon-pile load test)
From our workshop

A Toyota Hilux had an open circuit on the driver's knee airbag — diagnosed and fixed. We also rewired an old Toyota 700 from scratch.

03 / 11

Air Conditioning

Warm air on a Western Sydney summer day is no fun. We test the whole system to find out why — low gas, leaks, a blocked condenser or a failed compressor — then fix it properly.

  • A/C performance testing
  • Regas & leak detection
  • Compressor & condenser replacement
  • Blower & electrical faults
  • Cabin filter replacement
From our workshop

A customer's 2004 Suzuki had a faulty pump and a blocked condenser — the A/C is now working better than when it was new.

Good to know

Common questions

Do I need to book, or can I just turn up?

Booking is best so we can plan your job, but we always try to help when you're stuck — plenty of our customers have been fitted in the same day, including Saturday mornings. Call (02) 4732 3710 first.

Will you tell me the price before you start?

Yes. We diagnose the problem, explain what we found and give you a quote for approval before any repair work goes ahead.

Do you work on diesels, 4x4s and light trucks?

Absolutely — utes, 4x4s, light trucks and even farm machinery. Diesel diagnostics, injector servicing and decarbonising are regular jobs for us.

Do you service motorcycles?

Yes. We service and repair bikes and ATVs, and we specialise in dirt applications — MX and ATV.

What payment methods do you accept?

Debit and credit cards, including contactless (NFC) payments.
